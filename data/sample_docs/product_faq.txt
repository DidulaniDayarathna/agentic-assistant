Acme Robotics Product FAQ (Excerpt)

Q: What is the battery life of the Acme R1 vacuum robot?
A: The R1 runs for approximately 120 minutes on a full charge and
recharges fully in 3 hours via its docking station.

Q: Is the R1 compatible with voice assistants?
A: Yes, the R1 supports both Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for
start/stop/status voice commands.

Q: What is the warranty period?
A: All Acme Robotics products ship with a 2-year limited warranty
covering manufacturing defects.

Q: Can the R1 climb over thresholds or thick carpet?
A: The R1 can climb obstacles up to 2cm high, which covers most door
thresholds and medium-pile carpet.
