Acme Robotics Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

Remote Work Policy: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week,
subject to manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10am-3pm in
the employee's local timezone.

Vacation Policy: Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off
per year, plus 10 company holidays. Unused PTO up to 5 days rolls over
into the next calendar year.

Expense Reimbursement: Submit expense reports within 30 days of
purchase via the internal Expensify portal. Receipts are required for
any single expense over $25.

Equipment: New hires receive a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300
home-office stipend within their first month.
